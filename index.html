Featured story

I was in the middle of real financial trouble when Mick reached out. He didn't ask what I needed. He sent money in advance and hired me to work with his company — before I had done a thing to earn it.

Then he did the part that mattered more. He taught me. I learned Equitable Equity directly under his mentorship, and it changed the direction of my life.

Everything I have built since runs on what I learned from him. Every person who has walked away from a soulless job, every operator who found their footing using the AI-first system I built — none of it exists without one man deciding to help someone he had no obligation to help.

That's the thing about kindness. It never stops with the person who receives it.
Star RileyFounder, MyUSA Local
