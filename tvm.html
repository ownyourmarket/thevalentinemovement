TVM · The Valentine Movement Token

A movement is stronger when the people own a piece of it.

TVM exists for one reason: to give the community around this movement something to hold in common. Not a promise of profit — a symbol of participation, and a reason to keep showing up for each other.

Shared, not held

The people closest to the work should share in what it creates. That principle built every venture behind this movement, and TVM carries it into the community itself.

Morale you can see

Every movement needs a place where members can feel the momentum. TVM is a visible rallying point — proof that people are showing up, together, in the open.

Built by the team

Kindness scales when a community organizes around it. Holders, volunteers, partners, and storytellers are all building the same thing from different angles.
